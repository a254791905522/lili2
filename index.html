Christmas Grit Little Cheery

Festive hidden object fun

Free Puzzle iPhone & iPad 4+

Step into a magical holiday adventure. Christmas Grit Little Cheery is a cozy hidden object game full of warmth, joy, and festive charm. Explore hand-crafted Christmas scenes—from snug living rooms to snowy outdoor views—and spot cleverly tucked-away items before time runs out. Relax, sharpen your observation skills, and soak up a cheerful winter atmosphere at your own pace.

How you play

  • Search illustrated holiday levels for every object on your list.
  • Beat the clock on time-based challenges when you want extra focus.
  • Earn stars and unlock new levels as you clear each scene.
  • Enjoy a gentle, family-friendly pace—perfect for unwinding any time of year.

What makes it special

  • Many festive levels with difficulty that grows as you progress.
  • Colorful, illustrated scenes built for clarity and holiday mood.
  • Hidden object puzzles that reward attention to detail.
  • Optional hints and extras available through in-app purchases.
  • Game Center support for leaderboards where offered.
