1. Scope

This document summarizes privacy-related information for the iOS/iPadOS app Christmas Grit Little Cheery, distributed through Apple’s App Store. It is a plain-language companion to what appears on the store listing; Apple’s own policies also apply to your account, device, and store transactions.

2. Data collection

According to the app’s App Privacy details on the App Store, the product is reported as not collecting data for the categories Apple lists there. Practices can still depend on your iOS version, settings, and how you use system features (for example signing in to Game Center).

Summary: the published store disclosure indicates no app-side collection of personal data in Apple’s defined categories.

3. Apple & the App Store

Downloading, updating, billing, and Family Sharing are handled by Apple under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Review Apple’s materials if you want detail on Apple ID, payment data, or device analytics that operate at the platform level.

4. Advertising

The store listing notes that the app contains advertising. Ad networks may use their own technologies subject to Apple’s rules and your tracking preferences (such as App Tracking Transparency, where applicable). This page does not describe third-party ad SDKs in technical detail; their behavior may evolve with app updates.

5. Purchases & Game Center

Optional in-app purchases (for example hints or ad removal) are processed through Apple. If you use Game Center, leaderboard-related activity is governed by Apple’s Game Center terms and your privacy choices for that service.

6. Local storage on your device

Like most games, the app may keep progress, settings, or similar data locally on your iPhone or iPad so levels and preferences work offline. That information normally remains on the device unless a future version introduces explicit cloud sync, which would be described in release notes or store text when relevant.

7. Children

The app is rated suitable for ages 4 and up. Parents and guardians can use Apple’s Screen Time and purchase controls to manage installs and spending. For any concern about store-reported data practices, the App Store listing remains the primary reference.

8. Updates

This summary may be revised to match new store disclosures or features. The effective date at the top will change when the text is updated. If App Privacy labels or app behavior change, check the latest listing on the App Store for authoritative detail.
